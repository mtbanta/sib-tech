Summary

Strongest option: D, the hybrid. It scores 3.85 out of 5 against the weighted criteria, ahead of the always-on worker alone (3.60), the Relii-style web app (3.25), the hosted algorithm platform (3.15) and Claude in the chair (2.50).

The margin is thin and the pick is conditional. D beats C by a quarter of a point, and D is the hardest option for a non-coder to keep running. If operability is weighted above intraday fitness, B (the Relii-style web app) comes out first instead.
