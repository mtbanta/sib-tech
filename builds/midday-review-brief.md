# Mid-day session review brief (load-day morning, and normal mornings)

Written by the builder on 2026-11-09, evening (for the mid-day cycle, release 2), and rewritten on 2026-11-10, evening, at Michael's request ("Write builds/midday-review-brief.md for a load-day morning (including the first close-out try's request count against the limit, and any blocked sale with its logged quantities)"). It applies from the release of 2026-11-10 (the C2 log's "Release of 2026-11-10" and its deploy section say which Fly release runs). On a normal day, use the same sections and skip what is marked "load day". On a day the cycle runs by hand (Michael's fallback in CLAUDE.md), use the same sections with the manual steps.

**What a load day looks like since 2026-11-10 (Michael's decisions):** `load on` (before the morning screen, 7:45 AM Central) gives the cheap 30-name list. No entry before 8:45 AM. The load window runs from 8:45 AM to the 10:30 mid-day pause (not 9:00): 1-share limit buys at or below the price seen, take-profit 1 cent up, stop 2 cents down, cancelled if not filled in 5 seconds, up to the approved trades a minute within the 150 ceiling. Every entry is at most 75 dollars until the pause. At 10:30 the pause starts and the mid-day close-out sells to flat with about 30 positions open: this tests the uncapped first close-out try under load. After the pause the day is a normal day: the approved largest position, at most 100 orders a minute (an approved `trades 150` gives 100), the normal placeholder on the same cheap list, entries only after `go` or a deploy.

How to read it from the stack's records (8:30 to the mid-day close-out's end):

- Rate-limit errors: `rateLimitAnswers` in the per-minute statistics (`governor_stats`), and any 429 in the run log.
- Remaining against the reserve: `reserveThisMinute` (`lowestRemaining`, `lowestAboveReserve`) per minute; the lowest value, when, and what was spending then; the governor's "holding back for safety" refusals (`refusedByGovernor`, and the `refusals` table).
- Safety sales: `sale_blocked`, `liquidation_result`, `unprotected_position` and `bare_remainder` (with `listAgeMs`), and since 2026-11-10 `late_notice` (a case noticed more than 6 seconds after Alpaca's cancel) and `remainder_recount` (a remainder sale recounted because its take-profit filled more while its cancel landed); from Alpaca's records, every position whose exits were gone, for how long, and when it was sold. **Counting exits (Michael's ruling A, 2026-11-12):** an entry whose bracket take-profit leg shows cancelled and whose own stop leg then filled (Alpaca's one-cancels-the-other when the stop triggers) is a stop exit, never a "take-profit cancelled while the stop stayed live" or a lost take-profit chance; count it with the stops. Only a position left with neither exit working counts as lost exits. **Fresh stops (Michael's ruling B, 2026-11-12; the 15 second limit, its events and the cancel events below exist only while ruling B is on, `FRESH_STOP_LIMIT` in `src/risk.ts`; with it off, as on v20, an unclear fresh stop has no limit, no `fresh_stop_timeout*` event appears, and an unclear stop that the sync's list shows working is never confirmed from that list, so it ends as `fresh_stop_unclear_end` when its entry closes even though Alpaca held it; the other fresh stop events (`fresh_stop`, `fresh_stop_unclear`, `fresh_stop_confirmed`, `fresh_stop_lookup_failed`, `fresh_stop_gone`, `fresh_stop_recovered`, `fresh_stop_ended`, `fresh_stop_refused`) appear either way):** the fresh stops sent are every `fresh_stop` (confirmed by Alpaca's answer; `msToConfirm` is that answer's time) plus every `fresh_stop_unclear` with `sentToAlpaca` not false (answer unclear; one with `sentToAlpaca: false` was stopped by our own sell gate before any request and is reported apart, not counted as sent; leave every outcome event of such a stop, joined by its `stopClientOrderId`, out of the groups below). Each unclear one (joined by `stopClientOrderId`) ends in exactly one of: `fresh_stop_confirmed` (`found: true`, Alpaca had it; `afterLimit: true` means confirmed only after 15 seconds; `found: false`, Alpaca did not have it), `fresh_stop_timeout` (the 15 second limit: the stack then tries to cancel the stop if Alpaca holds it open and to sell the shares; the cancel may land late or not at all, so read its outcome from `fresh_stop_timeout_cancel` (sent and accepted), `fresh_stop_timeout_cancel_failed` (sent and not accepted) and `fresh_stop_timeout_cancel_not_sent` (the sale never sent it, for example it gave way to the loss stop's sale), each logged after the try with `by` (`15 second limit`, `missing-exit sale` or `sale`) and `again` (true when an earlier cancel of that stop was already accepted); `fresh_stop_timeout_still_open` marks a try that found the stop still open and holding the shares (its `status`; `retryInMs`, the backoff to the next try, on the branch where the sale was cancelling it or found it pending cancel); these events count tries, not requests (a try may send the cancel in more than one round), and the sale from the safety-sale events; its `found` says what the look at the limit saw: an open status or `filled` means Alpaca had it but it was not confirmed in time; `unknown_at_alpaca`, `not in Alpaca's open orders` or `lookup failed` means it was never confirmed), or `fresh_stop_unclear_end` (its entry closed or its shares left first). Report, so the groups add up to every stop sent: confirmed within 15 seconds (`fresh_stop`, plus `fresh_stop_confirmed` with `found: true` and `afterLimit: false`), confirmed after 15 seconds (`fresh_stop_confirmed` with `found: true` and `afterLimit: true`), Alpaca did not have it (`fresh_stop_confirmed` with `found: false`), cancelled at the limit (`fresh_stop_timeout`, by `found`), ended first (`fresh_stop_unclear_end`), each with its share of all fresh stops sent; give `fresh_stop_confirmed` counts by `status` too (a stop can come back cancelled or rejected). **Expected by design (not faults; report them, do not fail the morning for them):**
- From 10:30 until the mid-day close-out is flat, the missing-exit check stands aside (Michael, "Keep it"): positions whose exits the close-out cancelled are sold by the close-out alone. A position without exits during that time is not a delayed safety sale unless the close-out itself stalls.
- The close-out's retries (after the first try) run every 20 seconds, only within 60 Alpaca requests a minute and never below the reserve of 80, at most 10 positions a retry, in turn. Retries 20 seconds apart or slower are expected.
- While the close-out is selling, the stuck-entry 5 minute alert stands aside ("Mid-day owns it"); the close-out's own alerts are "the mid-day close-out is NOT FLAT yet" (once) and "still NOT FLAT after 5 minutes" (once).
- Since 2026-11-10 the close-out's wait for its own sales reads open orders on timed looks only (every 5th look, about every 10 seconds) and reads positions once more before ending, so a NOT FLAT alert while every remaining symbol has a sale of ours working is a fault (on 2026-11-10 it came after 544 ms).
- Since 2026-11-10 a cleanup sale reported as "Alpaca has not filled it yet. I will say when it has." gets one closing line when it fills (a remainder in the once-a-minute "Sold ... remainder" batch; a missing-exit sale "SYMBOL: the sale of its N shares has filled."). A "could not sell it yet" alert for a sale that had in fact sold everything left is a fault.
- On a load day, between 10:30:00 and the moment the pause is written (a few seconds), the 75 dollar cap still holds and the ceiling is already 100.
- If no deploy is planned that day, section 4 expects "resumed by `go`, or not at all".
