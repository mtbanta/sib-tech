# Mid-day session review brief (load-day morning, and normal mornings)

Written by the builder on 2026-11-09, evening (for the mid-day cycle, release 2), and rewritten on 2026-11-10, evening, at Michael's request ("Write builds/midday-review-brief.md for a load-day morning (including the first close-out try's request count against the limit, and any blocked sale with its logged quantities)"). It applies from the release of 2026-11-10 (the C2 log's "Release of 2026-11-10" and its deploy section say which Fly release runs). On a normal day, use the same sections and skip what is marked "load day". On a day the cycle runs by hand (Michael's fallback in CLAUDE.md), use the same sections with the manual steps.

**What a load day looks like since 2026-11-10 (Michael's decisions):** `load on` (before the morning screen: 8:15 AM Central since Michael's item 4 of 2026-11-13, 7:45 before) gives the cheap 30-name list. No entry before 8:45 AM. The load window runs from 8:45 AM to the 10:30 mid-day pause (not 9:00): 1-share limit buys at or below the price seen (at the price seen plus 1 cent once the release carrying Michael's ruling 3 runs; see "Since the morning build of 2026-11-17"), take-profit 1 cent up, stop 2 cents down, cancelled if not filled in 5 seconds, up to the approved trades a minute within the 150 ceiling. Every entry is at most 75 dollars until the pause. At 10:30 the pause starts and the mid-day close-out sells to flat with about 30 positions open: this tests the uncapped first close-out try under load. After the pause the day is a normal day: the approved largest position, at most 100 orders a minute (an approved `trades 150` gives 100), the normal placeholder on the same cheap list, entries only after `go` or a deploy.

**The close-out time is a setting (Michael's night build request of 2026-11-16, item 4).** The regular close-out runs `CLOSEOUT_MINUTES_BEFORE_CLOSE` minutes before the market close (the `[env]` section of `stacks/cyrus/fly.toml`; default 5, 2:55 PM Central; 15 for the test, 2:45 PM Central, or 11:45 AM on a half day). Take the value in force from the stored `worker_started` row in the run log (the `events` table, `closeoutMinutesBeforeClose`), not from Fly's log; an invalid value runs the default and stores a `setting_invalid` warning. The mid-day close-out at 10:30 is not affected; the retries never refused from 2:58 PM Central stay timed from the market close.
