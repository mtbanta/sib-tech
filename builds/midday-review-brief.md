# Mid-day session review brief (load-day morning, and normal mornings)

Written by the builder on 2026-11-09, evening (for the mid-day cycle, release 2), and rewritten on 2026-11-10, evening, at Michael's request ("Write builds/midday-review-brief.md for a load-day morning (including the first close-out try's request count against the limit, and any blocked sale with its logged quantities)"). It applies from the release of 2026-11-10 (the C2 log's "Release of 2026-11-10" and its deploy section say which Fly release runs). On a normal day, use the same sections and skip what is marked "load day". On a day the cycle runs by hand (Michael's fallback in CLAUDE.md), use the same sections with the manual steps.

**What a load day looks like since 2026-11-10 (Michael's decisions):** `load on` (before the morning screen, 7:45 AM Central) gives the cheap 30-name list. No entry before 8:45 AM. The load window runs from 8:45 AM to the 10:30 mid-day pause (not 9:00): 1-share limit buys at or below the price seen, take-profit 1 cent up, stop 2 cents down, cancelled if not filled in 5 seconds, up to the approved trades a minute within the 150 ceiling. Every entry is at most 75 dollars until the pause. At 10:30 the pause starts and the mid-day close-out sells to flat with about 30 positions open: this tests the uncapped first close-out try under load. After the pause the day is a normal day: the approved largest position, at most 100 orders a minute (an approved `trades 150` gives 100), the normal placeholder on the same cheap list, entries only after `go` or a deploy.

**Pass rule (Michael, 2026-11-09):** "report the highest accepted orders a minute; it holds if there are no rate-limit errors, remaining requests never drop below the reserve in force, no safety sale is delayed, and the mid-day close-out is flat by about 10:35." **Michael's ruling of 2026-11-06 on dips applies:** a dip caused only by cancels, syncs, loss-stop reads or sales is reported with its size but does not fail the morning, as long as there are no rate-limit errors and no safety sale is delayed. "No safety sale is delayed" is read in Michael's words: any sell, cancel, close-out or loss-stop sale that waited, for any reason (a rate limit, the governor, a stuck sale, a busy order sync), not only a rate limit.

How to read it from the stack's records (8:30 to the mid-day close-out's end):
- Accepted orders a minute: the stored entries per calendar minute and in the busiest 60 seconds, minus those Alpaca refused (fetch entries missing from Alpaca's day listing by client id before counting). Alpaca's paged order listing can repeat ids across pages: count distinct ids.
- Rate-limit errors: `rateLimitAnswers` in the per-minute statistics (`governor_stats`), and any 429 in the run log.
- Remaining against the reserve: `reserveThisMinute` (`lowestRemaining`, `lowestAboveReserve`) per minute; the lowest value, when, and what was spending then; the governor's "holding back for safety" refusals (`refusedByGovernor`, and the `refusals` table).
- Safety sales: `sale_blocked`, `liquidation_result`, `unprotected_position` and `bare_remainder` (with `listAgeMs`), and since 2026-11-10 `late_notice` (a case noticed more than 6 seconds after Alpaca's cancel) and `remainder_recount` (a remainder sale recounted because its take-profit filled more while its cancel landed); from Alpaca's records, every position whose exits were gone, for how long, and when it was sold.
- The mid-day close-out: the run log's `closeout_result` with `path: "midday"` (its `steps`: cancels sent and answered, each sale and its time; `waitedMs`), the day record's `midday` phase with `middayFlat: "yes"`, Alpaca's positions and open orders after it, and the Discord posts.

**Expected by design (not faults; report them, do not fail the morning for them):**
- From 10:30 until the mid-day close-out is flat, the missing-exit check stands aside (Michael, "Keep it"): positions whose exits the close-out cancelled are sold by the close-out alone. A position without exits during that time is not a delayed safety sale unless the close-out itself stalls.
- The close-out's retries (after the first try) run every 20 seconds, only within 60 Alpaca requests a minute and never below the reserve of 80, at most 10 positions a retry, in turn. Retries 20 seconds apart or slower are expected.
- While the close-out is selling, the stuck-entry 5 minute alert stands aside ("Mid-day owns it"); the close-out's own alerts are "the mid-day close-out is NOT FLAT yet" (once) and "still NOT FLAT after 5 minutes" (once).
- Since 2026-11-10 the close-out's wait for its own sales reads open orders on timed looks only (every 5th look, about every 10 seconds) and reads positions once more before ending, so a NOT FLAT alert while every remaining symbol has a sale of ours working is a fault (on 2026-11-10 it came after 544 ms).
- Since 2026-11-10 a cleanup sale reported as "Alpaca has not filled it yet. I will say when it has." gets one closing line when it fills (a remainder in the once-a-minute "Sold ... remainder" batch; a missing-exit sale "SYMBOL: the sale of its N shares has filled."). A "could not sell it yet" alert for a sale that had in fact sold everything left is a fault.
- On a load day, between 10:30:00 and the moment the pause is written (a few seconds), the 75 dollar cap still holds and the ceiling is already 100.
- If no deploy is planned that day, section 4 expects "resumed by `go`, or not at all".
