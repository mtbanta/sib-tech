**Pass rule (Michael, 2026-11-06), word for word:** "report the highest accepted orders a minute; it holds if there are no rate-limit errors, remaining requests never drop below the reserve in force, no safety sale is delayed, and the close-out is flat." For the mid-day cycle add "the mid-day close-out is flat by about 10:35" (Michael, 2026-11-09). **Michael's ruling of 2026-11-06 on dips:** a dip caused only by cancels, syncs, loss-stop reads or sales is reported with its size but does not fail the day, as long as there are no rate-limit errors and no safety sale is delayed. "No safety sale is delayed" is judged on the stack's own steps (Michael, 2026-11-11, night: "the pass rule judges our own steps"; "The stack's own part fails past 5 seconds of any wait the design does not list"). A sell, cancel, close-out or loss-stop sale fails the day when it waited on the stack (a rate limit, the governor, a stuck sale, a busy order sync, the worker's own slowness) for more than 5 seconds beyond the waits listed under "Expected by design". Waits inside Alpaca (a cancel Alpaca keeps "pending cancel", a sale Alpaca accepts but holds before submitting it, a market sale slow to fill, Alpaca's cancel of exits arriving late) do not fail the day; they are reported on the separate line below. (Before 2026-11-12 the reading was "for any reason", Michael's of 2026-11-06.)

How to read it from the stack's records:
- Accepted orders a minute: stored entries per calendar minute and in the busiest 60 seconds, minus those Alpaca refused.
- Rate-limit errors: `rateLimitAnswers` in `governor_stats` (one row a minute, covering the 60 seconds before it), and any 429 in the run log.
- Remaining against the reserve: `reserveThisMinute` (`lowestRemaining`, `lowestAboveReserve`), `lowestBudget`; the governor's holds (`refusedByGovernor`, the "holding back for safety" refusals).
- Safety sales: `sale_blocked`, `liquidation_result`, `unprotected_position`, `bare_remainder`, `late_notice` (a case noticed more than 6 seconds after Alpaca's cancel, with what the sync was doing), `remainder_recount` (a remainder sale recounted because its take-profit filled more while its cancel landed), `awaiting_fill_dropped`; and from Alpaca's records, every position whose exits were gone, for how long, and when it was sold. **Counting exits (Michael's ruling A, 2026-11-12):** an entry whose bracket take-profit leg shows cancelled and whose own stop leg then filled (Alpaca's one-cancels-the-other when the stop triggers) is a stop exit, never a "take-profit cancelled while the stop stayed live" or a lost take-profit chance; count it with the stops. Only a position left with neither exit working counts as lost exits. **Fresh stops (Michael's ruling B, 2026-11-12):** the fresh stops sent are every `fresh_stop` (confirmed by Alpaca's answer; `msToConfirm` is that answer's time) plus every `fresh_stop_unclear` with `sentToAlpaca` not false (answer unclear; one with `sentToAlpaca: false` was stopped by our own sell gate before any request and is reported apart, not counted as sent; leave every outcome event of such a stop, joined by its `stopClientOrderId`, out of the groups below). Each unclear one (joined by `stopClientOrderId`) ends in exactly one of: `fresh_stop_confirmed` (`found: true`, Alpaca had it; `afterLimit: true` means confirmed only after 15 seconds; `found: false`, Alpaca did not have it), `fresh_stop_timeout` (the 15 second limit: the stack then tries to cancel the stop if Alpaca holds it open and to sell the shares; the cancel may land late or not at all, so read its outcome from `fresh_stop_timeout_cancel` (accepted) and `fresh_stop_timeout_cancel_failed` (not accepted), each logged after the try, with `by` (`15 second limit`, `missing-exit sale` or `sale`; `again: true` marks a repeat of an accepted cancel, sent only on the stuck backoff since 2026-11-13), and the sale from the safety-sale events; its `found` says what the look at the limit saw: an open status or `filled` means Alpaca had it but it was not confirmed in time; `unknown_at_alpaca`, `not in Alpaca's open orders` or `lookup failed` means it was never confirmed), or `fresh_stop_unclear_end` (its entry closed or its shares left first). Report, so the groups add up to every stop sent: confirmed within 15 seconds (`fresh_stop`, plus `fresh_stop_confirmed` with `found: true` and `afterLimit: false`), confirmed after 15 seconds (`fresh_stop_confirmed` with `found: true` and `afterLimit: true`), Alpaca did not have it (`fresh_stop_confirmed` with `found: false`), cancelled at the limit (`fresh_stop_timeout`, by `found`), ended first (`fresh_stop_unclear_end`), each with its share of all fresh stops sent; give `fresh_stop_confirmed` counts by `status` too (a stop can come back cancelled or rejected). A worker restart forgets unclear stops without an outcome event: say so if one happened that day. Also report each timed-out stop whose cancel did not take effect at the first try (how many cancels, how long until the stop ended or the shares were sold) and any 5 minute alert saying a stop "is still resting at Alpaca".
- Total time without a stop (a separate line, not scored; Michael, 2026-11-11, night): for every position that went without a stop, from the moment its stop left (Alpaca's cancel, or the close-out's or loss stop's cancel) to the fill of its sale or a fresh stop accepted, split into the stack's part (from when the stack could know to when it sent the sale or stop) and Alpaca's part (a pending cancel, a held sale, the fill); then the day's worst. Report it in sections 1, 3, 4 and 6.
