Written by the builder on 2026-11-10, night, at Michael's request ("write builds/daily-review-brief.md as a reusable end-of-day brief (same shape as builds/monday-review-brief.md, covering the morning load window, the mid-day close-out, the afternoon, the 2:55 close-out, every alert that mentioned me, any blocked sale with its logged quantities, the summary against the account to the cent, and recommended fixes as a proposal only)"). It applies from Fly release v16 (the release of 2026-11-10); the C2 log says which release ran on the day. On a normal day, skip what is marked "load day". If a mid-day review was written that morning (`builds/midday-review-brief.md`), do not repeat its detail: cite it by heading and add what the afternoon changes.

**The close-out time is a setting (Michael's night build request of 2026-11-16, item 4: "The close-out time becomes a setting; set it to 2:45 PM Central for the test").** The regular close-out runs `CLOSEOUT_MINUTES_BEFORE_CLOSE` minutes before the market close: a whole number from 5 to 30, read at start-up from the `[env]` section of `stacks/cyrus/fly.toml` (default 5, 2:55 PM Central, or 11:55 AM on a half day; an invalid value runs the default and stores a `setting_invalid` warning in the run log). For the test it is 15: 2:45 PM Central, or 11:45 AM on a half day. Below, "the regular close-out" means that time: take the day's value from the `closeoutMinutesBeforeClose` field of the stored `worker_started` row in the run log (the `events` table), not from Fly's log. What moves with it: the close-out's start and retries, the emergency close-out (database down), the missing-exit check and the fast sale standing aside, the mid-day, afternoon and carried close-outs handing over, the loss stop's retry going quiet (one minute before the close-out), and the close-out time in the `load afternoon` reply and the `status` line. What stays timed from the market close: the retries never refused from 2:58 PM Central, the first try's wait for its own sales (until 2:58), the 20 second retries in the last minute, the last entry (2:00 PM), and the 3:05 PM daily summary.
